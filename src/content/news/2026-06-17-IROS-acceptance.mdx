"Safe Execution of RL Policies Via Acceleration-Based CBF-QP Constraint Enforcement for Real-World Robotic Deployments" was accepted at [IROS 2026](https://www.iros2026.org). The method filters a learned policy's commands at runtime through acceleration-based control barrier functions, enforcing position, velocity, torque and collision limits in a single quadratic program without retraining the policy.
